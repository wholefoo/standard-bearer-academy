### Technical Tip for Replit

Since you want **complete** courseware (PreK-12), the file size will eventually become huge.

* **Don't hardcode the lessons.** Instead, create a `lessons` folder in Replit and store each lesson as a **JSON or Markdown file**.
* Your app can then "pull" the content based on the grade level and subject. This makes it easy to update the worldview-specific language across the entire platform without digging through thousands of lines of code.
