Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Standard Bearer Academy ("we," "our," or "the Academy") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, particularly children and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information. Please read this policy carefully before using our platform.

By using Standard Bearer Academy, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

During the current beta phase, the platform does not require account creation or personal registration. However, the following information may be collected in the future when full LMS features are activated:

  • Names and email addresses of parents, teachers, and students
  • Student grade level and subject preferences
  • Contact information provided when reaching out to us directly

Automatically Collected Information

When you use our platform, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website addresses
  • General geographic location (country/region level only)

Quiz and Progress Data

The platform records quiz scores and lesson completion data. During the current beta period, this data is stored with a default anonymous identifier and is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the educational platform and curriculum
  • To track lesson completion and quiz scores for student progress
  • To improve and expand the curriculum based on usage patterns
  • To respond to inquiries and communications
  • To analyze platform usage through Google Analytics (see Section 5)
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for advertising targeting or commercial profiling.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Standard Bearer Academy is an educational platform designed for children under the supervision of their parents or legal guardians. We are committed to complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

During the current beta phase: No personal information is collected from children. All platform features are accessible without registration or account creation.

When full LMS features are activated: Any collection of personal information from children under 13 will require verifiable parental consent before any data is collected. Parents will have the right to review, modify, and delete their child's information at any time.

Parents who believe we may have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without proper consent should contact us immediately at boundlessvolumes@gmail.com.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take the security of your information seriously. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Quiz scores and progress data are stored in a secure PostgreSQL database. Access to this database is restricted and protected.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

5. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our platform. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to arriving. We use this information solely to improve our platform.

Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit, not your name or other identifying information. We do not combine the information collected through the use of Google Analytics with personally identifiable information.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookies

Our platform may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our site.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, some portions of our platform may not function properly.

7. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or resources. These links are provided for your convenience and informational purposes only. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of those sites and are not responsible for their privacy policies or practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at boundlessvolumes@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the platform after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
